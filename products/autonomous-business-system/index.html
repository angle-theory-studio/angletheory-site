THE FOUR DEPARTMENTS

MANAGEMENT AND CONTROL

SALES

Organises the path from finding a prospect to a deal: qualification, communication, proposals and follow-ups.

MARKETING

Analyses the market and your audience, helps shape positioning, prepare content and campaigns, and pass signals of interest to sales.

THE MAIN ADVANTAGE

Departments work with shared data and pass tasks to one another without losing context. This reduces manual coordination and helps you oversee the entire journey — from the first signs of demand to meeting commitments to the customer.
